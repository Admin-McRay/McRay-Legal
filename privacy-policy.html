Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

McRay Roofing ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our internal SMS-based billing confirmation system used to communicate with our own sales representatives.

1. Information We Collect

In connection with this SMS program, we collect and process:

  • Mobile phone numbers of our sales representatives
  • The content of text message conversations related to confirming job costs (materials, labor, and other job-related expenses)
  • Job and billing information associated with those conversations (e.g. job name, estimated and actual costs)

We do not collect this information from the general public or from customers — this program is used solely for internal communication with our own employees/contractors.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through this program is used only to:

  • Confirm actual job costs after work is completed
  • Generate internal billing reports for our accounting/billing staff
  • Maintain accurate job records in our business systems (JobNimbus)

We do not use this information for marketing, and we do not sell or rent this information to any third party.

3. How Information Is Shared

We share information only with service providers who help us operate this system, under obligations to protect it:

  • Twilio (SMS delivery)
  • Anthropic (Claude AI, used to process and interpret message content)
  • JobNimbus (our business's CRM/job management system)
  • SendGrid (email delivery of internal billing reports)

We do not share this information with any other third party except as required by law.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties for any purpose.

4. Data Retention

Message content and related job cost data are retained as part of our normal business recordkeeping (within JobNimbus) for as long as reasonably necessary for accounting and business purposes.

5. Your Choices

Employees/contractors participating in this program may opt out of receiving these messages at any time by replying STOP, or by contacting us directly (see Section 6).
