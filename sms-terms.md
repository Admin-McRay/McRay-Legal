## Program Description

McRay Roofing operates an internal SMS-based billing confirmation program used to communicate with our own sales representatives after a job is marked complete, in order to confirm actual job costs (materials, labor, and other expenses) before billing.

This program is for internal business use only and is not used to send marketing messages or to communicate with customers or the general public.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and is based on job completion activity — typically one initial message per completed job, plus any follow-up clarifying messages needed to confirm costs.

## Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on the recipient's mobile carrier and plan.
